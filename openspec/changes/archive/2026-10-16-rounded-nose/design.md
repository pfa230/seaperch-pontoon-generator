# Design

With cone half-angle a = 35°, body radius R and mount radius r, keep the cone's virtual point at z = L. The front sphere remains centred at L − R / sin a. Put the nose sphere at L − r / sin a so both spheres are tangent to the same cone. Each join has polar angle 55°: radial coordinate radius × cos a and axial offset radius × sin a. The rounded body ends at L − r / sin a + r.

Revolve one sphere–cone–sphere outline and fuse the existing mount cylinder at the nose sphere's centre. The sphere contains the cylinder's base disc at every permitted angle, removing the need for a separately fused ball. A coaxial cylinder covers the spherical pole, so that pole creates no spike inside the mount.

Keep the existing pointed inward outline and intersect it with the existing 35° cavity-cap cone, placing the cone point at min(screw-hole lowest point, rounded body tip) − t. This cap lies inside the inward-offset nose sphere, so drawing an inner spherical arc would add geometry that the intersection always removes. The cavity roof stays conical. L keeps its existing virtual-point reference, avoiding unrelated changes to body diameter, cylinder length or the minimum accepted length.
