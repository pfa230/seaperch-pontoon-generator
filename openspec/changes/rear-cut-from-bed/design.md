# Design

## Decisions

### The cavity's rear dome is not an overhang

The inner rear sphere stays the concentric inward offset of the outer rear sphere, so the wall there is exactly t. Its surface faces up into the cavity and rests on the wall beneath it, so it is not an overhang and the 35° limit does not apply to it, even where it leans about 53.6° from vertical at the default cavity floor. The overhang limit governs downward-facing surfaces only: the outer rear band below the 35° latitude is the one exception, and every downward-facing cavity surface (the front dome and the cone cap) keeps the 35° limit. The proposal's statement that the cavity keeps the 35° limit means those downward-facing surfaces.
